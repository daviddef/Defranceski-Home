Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026. This page covers defranceski.com only.

You do not need an account, and nothing on this page asks you for your name or email. Here is what is counted, and what Google's ads can do.

The count at the foot of the page

The “times read” number comes from GoatCounter, a privacy-minded counter. It sets no cookies and does not follow you from site to site. It records that a page was read, and roughly the kind of browser and the country, not who you are.

Ads

This site is set up to show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to choose and measure ads, including ads based on your interests. Where the law asks for it, as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google shows a choice before any of that happens, and you can change your mind later.

  • To see and turn off personalised ads: Google Ad Settings.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Fonts

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google’s servers for them, and Google sees the usual details of that request, such as your IP address.

The family archives

The family archive sites, such as the Defranceschi, Falco and Lerena archives, are separate sites. They show no ads. They also never name a living person: that rule is how those archives are written.
